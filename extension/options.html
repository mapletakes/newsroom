The Broadside Quick Add

Paste your add token from The Broadside → Settings → Quick add. Then use the toolbar button or right-click “Add to The Broadside” on any page, link, or video.

Right-click a page, link, or video for the full menu — every deck segment and shelf. The toolbar button always uses this default, so you can point it at a shelf while researching and flip it back to the deck once you're live.
